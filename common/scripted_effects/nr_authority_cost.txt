# NR: timed authority cost of our negotiation options (pattern "Authority cost slots" in README_NR.md).
# Ten slots (static modifiers nr_authority_cost_1..10, country_authority_add = -1 times the cost), so several
# costs running at once never overwrite each other. Check nr_authority_cost_slot_free before offering an option.
